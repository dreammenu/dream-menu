Privacy notice

Your data and this site

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Dream Menu is a hospitality interview series and intelligence platform based in Glasgow, run by Gordon Best. Contact us through @dream.menu on Instagram.

Interview answers

When you take part, we keep your answers (dishes, venues and comments) and the details you give us to contact you. We publish answers on this site and in our reports, and we share coded, aggregated data with business partners under written agreements. Quotes on the map are shown without your name. Ask us at any time to see, correct or remove your answers.

Visiting the site

  • Public pages do not use cookies. Partner pages use one sign-in cookie, set only when a partner signs in.
  • The rankings page remembers the last market you picked in your own browser. It never leaves your device.
  • Fonts and map code are served from this site. When you zoom the map in to city level, street map images load from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address to send them.
  • Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits: it sets no cookies, does not track you across sites and only gives us totals such as pages viewed, referring sites, country and device type.
  • The enquiry form on the For business page does not send or store anything on this site. It opens an email in your own mail app, and we only receive what you choose to send.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
