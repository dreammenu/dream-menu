Press and researchers

Press kit

Dream Menu asks chefs, creators and people who love eating out one question: build your dream menu, one dish per course, from places you actually go. 1,256 interviews later, these are the figures most worth quoting, how to credit them and who to call.

Figures worth quoting

From 1,256 Dream Menu interviews, September 2025 to 10 November 2026. Each card copies the figure with its source line.

46%choose Irn-Bru over Coca-Cola (1,229 answers)
51%say no to pineapple on pizza: the closest debate on the menu
67%pick coffee over tea (957 answers)
35%ask for tap water with dinner, close behind still at 37%
12%name tiramisu as their dream dessert, the most-named single dish on any course
8%choose scallops to start
2.6%of Edinburgh venue mentions are for the negroni, against 0.7% across all markets

123venues guests name also hold a MICHELIN, 50 Best or guide award

Glasgow’s most-named venue is Crabshakk Finnieston (2.2% of the city’s venue mentions). Tagine and Middle Eastern takes 0.5% of Glasgow’s venue mentions, against 0.3% across all markets. More on the food trends page, the analysis and the rankings.

How to credit

Please credit Dream Menu (dream-menu.com) and give the base and date, for example: Source: Dream Menu, 1,256 interviews to 10 November 2026. Link to the page the figure came from where you can.

The method in brief

Guests answer the same questions through Instagram: a starter, main, side, dessert and drinks, and the venue that does each best, plus a hidden gem and four debates. Answers are coded by hand to 348 dish categories. Venues are matched and placed on the map (1,722 of 1,935 pinned). It is qualitative fieldwork with a large base, not a statistically representative survey: guests are weighted towards Glasgow and towards people who work in or write about food. Movement over time is only called when it passes a two-proportion test. Full method.
