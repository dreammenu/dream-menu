What is moving

Prepared forDunns Food & Drinks

1,250 interviews with dish answers. A move is only called clear when the pooled share of the latest three quarters (2026-Q2, 2026-Q3, 2026-Q4) differs from the first two (2025-Q3, 2025-Q4) under a two-proportion z-test, p < 0.05. Every dish sits in a parent group (scallops in seafood, tiramisu in tiramisu and creamy desserts), and both levels are tested: a group can move clearly even when no single dish in it does. Quarters with fewer than 100 answers are drawn hollow. Soft and alcoholic drinks are now reported separately.

Trend scorecard

Every tracked dish and drink scored out of 10: demand (how many guests choose it), momentum (how fast it is moving), evidence (how sure the move is) and room to enter (whether one venue owns it), less a penalty if guests also want it binned. Back means act now, Watch means test it, Review means it is fading or crowded.

Who is answering

Guests grouped from their own introductions: industry (chefs, owners, staff, suppliers), creators (food pages and reviewers) and enthusiasts (everyone else). The group is now stored against each interview in the sheet, in the Respondent group column. Shares are of all dish answers in each group.
