What is moving

Prepared forDunns Food & Drinks

1,215 interviews with dish answers. A move is only called clear when the pooled share of the latest three quarters (2026-Q2, 2026-Q3, 2026-Q4) differs from the first two (2025-Q3, 2025-Q4) under a two-proportion z-test, p < 0.05. Every dish sits in a parent group (scallops in seafood, tiramisu in tiramisu and creamy desserts), and both levels are tested: a group can move clearly even when no single dish in it does. Quarters with fewer than 100 answers are drawn hollow. Soft and alcoholic drinks are now reported separately.
