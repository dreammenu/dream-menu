What the partnership includes

3.1

Quarterly Industry Insight Review

A written report each quarter, built around the commercial questions agreed at the start of the quarter. If no questions are agreed within 14 days, Dream Menu sets the scope from the previous quarter. The review of October to December 2026 follows the end of the quarter.

Quarterly presentation

One presentation a quarter to Dunns management and sales teams, in person or remotely, with recommendations and actions.

Terms of use

  • For Dunns' own internal sales, buying, marketing and strategic use only. The licence is non-exclusive and non-transferable.
  • The quarterly reviews, data extracts and map data are confidential. Do not redistribute or publish them outside Dunns without Dream Menu's written consent.
  • Map access is for named Dunns users and does not include any right to republish, resell or embed the platform or its rankings.
  • Dunns may mention the partnership in its own marketing but may not reproduce review content externally without consent.
  • Data is aggregated and anonymised. Dream Menu may withhold or redact material that participants have not consented to share.
  • The insight comes from qualitative fieldwork, given in good faith. It is not a complete or statistically representative picture of the market.
  • Access to the map and partner area ends if the partnership ends. Reviews already delivered can be kept for internal reference.

Summary only. The signed partnership terms govern.

Sponsorship on Dream Menu output
