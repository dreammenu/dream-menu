How it works

What every report includes

  • 01

    The answer first

    A one-sentence answer to the client's question, three findings with the number behind each, and a ranked list of what to do.

  • 02

    Evidence you can check

    Numbered exhibits with the data table under each, named venues and dishes, and the method in an appendix.

  • 03

    Recommendations by impact

    Each action placed by impact and effort, with timing, and the signals that would change the view.

  • 04

    Quarterly follow-up

    Optional tracking each quarter, so the next report shows what moved and whether it was a clear move.
