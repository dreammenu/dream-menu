Every number here comes from 1,238 Dream Menu interviews, September 2025 to 29 October 2026. Dish shares use the 1,232 interviews that answered the dish questions. Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026, and each dish sits in a parent group such as seafood or cakes and bakes.
