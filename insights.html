Every number here comes from 1,256 Dream Menu interviews, September 2025 to 10 November 2026. Dish shares use the 1,250 interviews that answered the dish questions. Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026, and each dish sits in a parent group such as seafood or cakes and bakes.

Each card is one course: first the share of answers by food group, then by single dish. Bars are scaled to the top item and the figure is the true share.

Groups and dishes are two cuts of the same answers, so their figures will not match.
