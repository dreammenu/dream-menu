About Dream Menu

Foodies, speaking for themselves

Dream Menu asks one question: what is on your dream menu, and where would you eat it? We put it to chefs, owners, front of house, suppliers, writers and foodies of every kind. Their answers become free food trends, a map of the venues they name, and evidence that helps hospitality make better calls.

1,340 interviews
1,740 venues named
30 countries

Why we do it

Most of what the trade knows about demand comes from tills, delivery apps and guides. Tills show what sold from the menu a venue already had. Guides show what critics rate. Neither asks guests what they would actually choose if they could have anything, from anywhere.

That is the gap Dream Menu fills. Every guest builds the same menu, one dish per course plus drinks, and names the place that does each one best. Asked the same way every time, those answers add up to a steady, comparable read of what people want and where they think it is done well.

It started in Glasgow as an Instagram interview series in September 2025, grown out of the city’s food and hospitality community. It now takes answers from across the UK and beyond.

Ways to work with us

How we keep it honest

  • Guests in their own words. We report what people said, not what anyone would like them to say. Sponsors do not change the findings.
  • Shares, not volumes. Some cities have far more answers than others, so we compare percentages.
  • Clear about limits. This is qualitative fieldwork with a large base, weighted towards Glasgow and people who work in food. A move is only called when it passes a statistical test.
  • Checked against others. Findings are benchmarked against national polls, menu trackers and sales data, and we say where we agree and where we do not.
  • Names only by choice. Guests are published by name only if they ask to be. Everyone else is counted anonymously.
