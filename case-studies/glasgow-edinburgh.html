Two cities, two appetites

Written for a restaurant group with a Glasgow site, weighing a first opening in Edinburgh (or the reverse). The question: how differently do the two cities eat and drink, and what would a second site need to change?

The brief

A group running a successful Glasgow venue wants to know whether the format travels 45 miles east. Menus, drinks lists and launch marketing all depend on how far Edinburgh guests differ, so the report compares what guests in each city name, which venues set the bar, and how concentrated each market is.

What we found

Edinburgh drinks stirred, Glasgow drinks shaken

Edinburgh guests name the negroni at 346 and the martini at 280 on the index (100 = the all-market average). In Glasgow they index at 61 and 58, while the margarita indexes at 154. A Glasgow bar list built on margaritas would need a stirred, spirit-forward section in Edinburgh.

Glasgow is the scallop city

Scallops are 3.3% of Glasgow dish mentions (index 127) against 1.3% in Edinburgh (index 50). Edinburgh still loves seafood (10.6% of its venue mentions against 9.3% in Glasgow), but it is led by Leith fish venues rather than one shellfish dish.

Pudding beats tiramisu in Edinburgh

Sticky toffee pudding indexes at 152 in Edinburgh and 90 in Glasgow; tiramisu runs the other way (127 in Glasgow, 80 in Edinburgh). The dessert that sells in one city is not the safe bet in the other.

Edinburgh is more concentrated

Edinburgh's ten most-named venues take 23% of the city's 583 venue mentions; Glasgow's top ten take 16% of 2,029. A newcomer in Edinburgh is competing with a shorter list of strong favourites, led by Hawksmoor Edinburgh.

Method and limits

Built from venue mentions in 1,238 Dream Menu interviews to 29 October 2026. Edinburgh has far fewer mentions than Glasgow (382 against 1,293 dish mentions), so single-dish figures there rest on 5 to 10 mentions each and are directional.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, not a statistically representative survey. How Dream Menu works.
