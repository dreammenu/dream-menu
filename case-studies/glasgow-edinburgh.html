No. 02Dream Menu Insight Series · Market-entry read

Two cities, two appetites

A Glasgow format that works is not guaranteed to work 45 miles east. This read compares what guests in each city name, which venues set the bar, and how concentrated each market is.

Written for
A restaurant group with a Glasgow venue, weighing an Edinburgh opening
The question
What would a second site need to change?
Status
Provisional sample, 29 October 2026
Evidence
1,675 dish mentions, 2,612 venue mentions
346Edinburgh negroni index (100 = average)
154Glasgow margarita index
23%of Edinburgh mentions go to its top 10 venues

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

The format can travel, but the bar list, the dessert and the launch plan should not: Edinburgh drinks stirred, prefers pudding and concentrates its loyalty on fewer venues.

  • 346index

    Edinburgh drinks stirred, Glasgow shaken

    Negroni 346 and martini 280 in Edinburgh; the margarita indexes 154 in Glasgow.

  • 127index

    Glasgow is the scallop city

    Scallops index 127 in Glasgow and 50 in Edinburgh, where white fish leads instead.

  • 23%top-10 share

    Edinburgh is more concentrated

    Its ten favourites take 23% of mentions against 16% in Glasgow.

What we recommend

  1. Rewrite the bar list for Edinburgh
  2. Plan launch PR around the favourites
  3. Test the format before signing
  4. Swap the dessert anchor
  5. Lead starters with named Scottish fish

02 · Dish by dish

Edinburgh drinks stirred; Glasgow shakes and orders shellfish

Each city's share of dish mentions against the all-market average, and where the two differ most.

So what for a second site

The bar list is the biggest change: Edinburgh names the negroni at 346 and the martini at 280 on the index; Glasgow at 61 and 58. A margarita-led list needs a stirred section to travel east.

03 · Who sets the bar

Edinburgh's loyalty sits with fewer venues

The venues each city names most, and how much of the conversation they hold.

So what for a newcomer

In Edinburgh you are competing with a short list of strong favourites led by Hawksmoor Edinburgh. Aim to be talked about alongside them, not instead of them.

04 · Signature dishes

The dessert that sells in one city is second choice in the other

Four dishes that change between the cities.

Edinburgh still loves seafood: it is 10.6% of the city's venue mentions against 9.3% in Glasgow. But where Glasgow names scallops (3.3% of dish mentions, index 127), Edinburgh names white fish and Leith's fish specialists. On dessert the cities swap: sticky toffee pudding indexes 152 in Edinburgh, tiramisu 127 in Glasgow.

05 · Recommendations

What to do

Ranked by impact. The matrix shows where to start; the list says why.

  1. 01

    Rewrite the bar list for Edinburgh

    Keep two margaritas and add a stirred section (negroni, martini, a freezer martini). Negroni index 346 in Edinburgh against 61 in Glasgow.

    Impact: HighEffort: LowBefore opening
  2. 02

    Plan launch PR around the favourites

    Ten venues take 23% of Edinburgh mentions. Collaborations or guest nights with them earn word of mouth faster than paid launch media.

    Impact: HighEffort: MediumLaunch quarter
  3. 03

    Test the format before signing

    Twenty interviews with Edinburgh guests on your format and dishes before committing to a site: cheap insurance on a six-figure fit-out.

    Impact: HighEffort: MediumSite search
  4. 04

    Swap the dessert anchor

    Sticky toffee pudding indexes 152 in Edinburgh; tiramisu 127 in Glasgow. Lead with the local favourite.

    Impact: MediumEffort: LowBefore opening
  5. 05

    Lead starters with named Scottish fish

    Keep one shellfish signature, but Edinburgh guests reward white fish and Leith-style fish cookery more than scallops.

    Impact: MediumEffort: MediumMenu build

06 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Edinburgh sample size

Edinburgh has 382 dish mentions against 1,293 in Glasgow. A commission would add targeted Edinburgh interviews to firm up single-dish reads.

Festival distortion

August interviews include Fringe visitors. Excluding August would show whether the stirred-drinks lean is local or visitor-led.

Cocktail fatigue

Cocktails are losing share across the archive (No. 05). Stirred classics are holding up better than house cocktails.

New favourites

A new Edinburgh opening entering the top ten would show the market is less closed than it looks.

07 · Appendix

Method and sources

1,293Glasgow dish mentions
382Edinburgh dish mentions
818Glasgow venues named
290Edinburgh venues named

Built from venue mentions in 1,238 interviews to 29 October 2026. The index compares a city's share of dish mentions with the share across all markets (100 = average). Only dishes with enough mentions in both cities are shown; single-dish figures for Edinburgh rest on 5 to 10 mentions each and are directional.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
