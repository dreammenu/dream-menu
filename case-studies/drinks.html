What Scotland drinks with dinner

Written for a drinks brand or wholesaler planning its on-trade range. The question: is the cocktail boom over, what is replacing it, and what goes in the soft-drink round?

The brief

A drinks business wants to know where the on-trade is heading in Scotland. The report reads the dream alcoholic drink over time, the soft drinks guests choose, and the debates that every interview ends with.

What we found

Cocktails are losing share

Cocktails fell from 72.1% to 53.8% of dream alcoholic drinks, the largest clear move in the archive. They are still the biggest group, but the gap is closing.

Whisky, lager and spirits are taking it

Spirits rose from 4.8% to 13.6%, led by whisky (3.1% to 9.6%). Beer and cider rose from 7.9% to 13.3%, with lager clearly up. All three are clear moves.

Irn Bru wins the round

555 guests choose Irn Bru, 405 Coca-Cola and 251 neither (1,211 answering). In Scotland, a soft-drinks list without Irn Bru is missing the favourite.

No and low is a list, not a line

12.4% of dream soft drinks are mocktails or alcohol-free, and mocktails sit in the top five dream drinks overall. Guests who choose them name a specific drink and venue, so one token option undersells it.

Method and limits

Built from 908 codable alcoholic drink answers and 411 soft drink answers in 1,238 Dream Menu interviews to 29 October 2026, plus the debate questions every interview ends with.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, not a statistically representative survey. How Dream Menu works.
