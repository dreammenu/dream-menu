What Scotland drinks with dinner

Is the cocktail boom over? This report reads the dream alcoholic drink over time, the soft drinks guests choose, and the debates every interview ends with.

Written for
A drinks brand or wholesaler planning its on-trade range
The question
Where is the on-trade drink heading in Scotland?
Status

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

The cocktail boom is cooling, not over: guests are trading the long house list for whisky, lager and the classics, and the soft round in Scotland starts with Irn Bru.

  • −18.2pts

    Cocktails are losing share

    From 72.1% to 53.8% of dream alcoholic drinks: the biggest clear move in the archive.

  • +8.8pts

    Spirits and beer are taking it

    Spirits 4.8% to 13.6%, led by whisky; lager clearly up too.

  • 46%Irn Bru

    Irn Bru wins the round

    555 choose Irn Bru, 405 Coca-Cola, 251 neither.

What we recommend

  1. Cut the house cocktail list; keep the classics
  2. Sell whisky by the dram and the highball
  3. Put a premium or local lager on draught
  4. List Irn Bru and price soft drinks as margin
  5. Build three named alcohol-free serves

02 · The alcoholic drink

Cocktails are giving ground to whisky and lager

Early against recent, and quarter by quarter.

So what for a drinks range

Cocktails are still the biggest group (53.8% recently) but the long house list is the casualty. Whisky (3.1% to 9.6%) and lager (2.2% to 6.3%) are the clear gainers.

03 · The soft round

Irn Bru, coffee and still water win the debates

What guests choose when the drink is not alcoholic.

05 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Drink question split

Since 23 August 2026 soft and alcoholic drinks are asked separately. Two quarters on the new questions will confirm the trend on a cleaner base.

Negroni and martini

Stirred classics are rising without a clear move yet (see No. 02 for the Edinburgh lean).

Guinness

Guinness and stout drifted down. A recovery in winter quarters would be seasonal, not structural.

No and low

No and low fell as a share of soft drinks. Watch whether that is fatigue or an effect of the new question.

06 · Appendix

Method and sources

908alcoholic drink answers
411soft drink answers
1,211Irn Bru or Coke answers
5quarters tested

Alcoholic and soft drink shares pool the original single drink question and the two questions that replaced it on 23 August 2026; trend tests use the original question only, so the latest quarter is left out of the trend charts.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
