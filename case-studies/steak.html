The steak economy

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

Steak is the biggest main course idea and still growing, but guests are moving from fillet to flavour cuts, braises and the steak pie, so the range should follow the flavour.

  • 25.5%of mains

    Beef leads the main course

    316 of 1,237 dream mains, rising from 21.9% to 27.2% (not yet a clear move).

  • +2.3pts

    The steak pie is back

    From 0.7% to 3.0% of mains: a clear move.

  • −0.9pts

    Fillet is slipping

    Short rib and ribeye rise as fillet falls: guests are trading to flavour.

What we recommend

  1. Lead the range with flavour cuts
  2. Supply a premium steak pie
  3. Package braises for small kitchens
  4. Sell the plate, not just the cut
  5. Target the venues guests already name

03 · The cuts

Guests are trading from fillet to flavour

Early against recent, and quarter by quarter.

So what for a beef supplier

Guests are trading towards flavour: short rib (2.3% to 4.4%), ribeye (1.6% to 3.3%) and steak pie (0.7% to 3.0%, a clear move) are rising while fillet slips (3.9% to 3.0%). Those are typically the cuts with the better margin per plate.

04 · The plate and the venues

Greens are the new steak side

The venues guests name for beef, and what goes beside it.

So what for venues

The steak plate is changing around the meat: greens and creamed spinach rose from 3.0% to 6.2% of dream sides, a clear move. Steak tartare and carpaccio hold 2.9% of starters.

06 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Beef prices

If wholesale beef prices rise, the shift to braises and pies may speed up; a commission would pair this read with price data.

The beef group

Beef mains are up +5.3 points without a clear move yet (p = 0.084). One more strong quarter would confirm it.

Fillet

A continued fall would make fillet a special-occasion cut rather than a menu staple.

Plant-based mains

Vegetable sides are rising. Watch whether that reaches the main course and takes share from beef.

Method and sources

316beef and steak mains

Main and side shares use interviews with a codable answer for that course. "Steak (cut not named)" covers answers naming steak without a cut.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
