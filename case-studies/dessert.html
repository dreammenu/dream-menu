The last spend: dessert

Dessert is the course guests cut first when money is tight. This report asks which desserts still earn their place, what is moving, and what to stop making.

Written for
A dessert maker or foodservice supplier
The question
Which desserts still earn their place on the bill?

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

Two classics carry the volume and fruit is the clear riser, so a dessert range should lead with tiramisu and sticky toffee pudding, add a seasonal fruit line, and offer a smaller format so the course stays on the bill.

  • 23.1%of desserts

    Two classics carry the course

    Tiramisu and sticky toffee pudding, both steady over six quarters.

  • +3.5pts

    Fruit is the clear riser

    Fruit and meringue rose from 2.6% to 6.1%; baked cheesecake fell 3.9 points.

  • 105mentions

    Novelty chocolate is tired

    Dubai chocolate is the second most-named trend guests want gone.

What we recommend

  1. Make tiramisu and sticky toffee pudding the volume lines
  2. Offer a smaller dessert at a lower price
  3. Add a seasonal fruit and meringue line
  4. Reduce baked cheesecake; test a Basque style
  5. Do not build a range on viral flavours

02 · The course

Two classics, then an open field

What guests choose for dessert, and how fragmented the course is against the others.

So what for a dessert range

The volume is in two classics; the opportunity is the open space after them. No other dessert has more than 5% of answers, so a venue can still make one its own.

03 · What is moving

Fruit is up, cheesecake down, novelty chocolate tired

Early against recent, and what guests want gone.

04 · The spend

The last spend walks next door

What operators see, and where guests go instead.

So what for restaurants

Only 9 of 54 operators see spend per head rising. When a venue's own dessert is forgettable, guests take the last spend to the gelato shop next door.

06 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Spend per head

If the next operator survey shows spend per head recovering, the smaller-format dessert matters less and premium lines matter more.

Fruit and meringue

Fruit is seasonal. Two more quarters including winter will show whether the rise holds outside summer.

Chocolate desserts

Chocolate desserts are drifting down (13.9% to 9.6%) without a clear move yet.

Late-night dessert

A documented concept gap in Glasgow. A late-night dessert venue gaining mentions would confirm demand.

07 · Appendix

Method and sources

1,206dream desserts
55operators surveyed
497"bin it" mentions (top 10)
6quarters tracked

Dessert shares use interviews with a codable dessert answer. The operator survey ran 2 April to 3 May 2026. "Bin it" answers are unprompted and grouped into published labels.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
