Glasgow's Italian table

Glasgow has loved Italian food for a century. This report shows where it sits on the dream menu today, from the tiramisu to the takeaway pizza, and how the venues that win are doing it.

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

Italian is on every course and owns dessert, but the share is steady, not growing, so the venues that win are specialists with one thing done better than anyone: fresh pasta, a famous tiramisu, a clear point of view.

04 · Inside Italian

So what for an Italian operator

The Italian share is steady rather than growing (8.5% to 8.4% of mains), so growth comes from taking share within it. The venues that win are specialists: fresh pasta (Sugo), one great tiramisu (Celino's), a clear point of view (Margo).

06 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Pasta share

Pasta and Italian mains are steady at 8.6%. A fall would suggest saturation in the city centre.

Tiramisu fatigue

Tiramisu is so dominant it could tire. Watch the "bin it" list for it.

Pizza

Pizza on dream mains is small and steady; its strength is comfort and takeaway.

Edinburgh

Tiramisu indexes lower in Edinburgh, where sticky toffee pudding leads (see No. 02).

A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
