Scotland's seafood appetite

Seafood is the single biggest idea on Scotland's dream menu. This report reads it across starters and mains, tracks it quarter by quarter, and names the venues guests associate with it.

Written for
A Scottish seafood supplier planning its foodservice range
The question
Which seafood products should we push next year, and to whom?
Status
Provisional sample, 31 October 2026
Evidence
1,207 starters, 1,219 mains, venue mentions
36.0%of dream starters are seafood
8.0%name scallops, the top starter
15.1%of dream mains are seafood
+3.3 ptswhite fish mains, a clear rise

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

Seafood owns the starter and is growing on the main course, so the range should pair a named-origin scallop hero with a white fish line for mains, sold to chefs first.

  • 36.0%of starters

    Seafood owns the starter

    1,207 dream starters; seafood takes 36.0%, and scallops alone 8.0%.

  • +3.3pts

    White fish is the riser

    Cod, hake and white fish mains rose from 2.3% to 5.6%, a clear move.

  • 12.4%of trade picks

    Chefs are the advocates

    Hospitality professionals name seafood more than any other group.

What we recommend

  1. Make named-origin scallops the hero line
  2. Build a white fish range for mains
  3. Sell to chefs first
  4. Help one venue per district own a seafood dish
  5. Track the starter share quarterly

02 · The starter

Seafood takes a third of all dream starters

Where seafood sits against every other food group, and which dishes lead.

03 · The trend

The growth is moving to the main course

Early against recent, and quarter by quarter.

So what for a seafood supplier

The volume is in starters; the growth is in mains. White fish rose from 2.3% to 5.6% of dream mains, a clear move, while starters hold steady around a third.

04 · Where and who

One venue owns it; the trade champions it

The venues guests name for seafood, and which guests name it most.

So what for a supplier

Guests repeat provenance back word for word: hand-dived, Orkney, Leith. Named origin is the selling point, and outside Crabshakk Finnieston no venue has yet claimed a seafood dish as its own.

05 · Recommendations

What to do

Ranked by impact. The matrix shows where to start; the list says why.

  1. 01

    Make named-origin scallops the hero line

    Scallops are 8.0% of all starters, the single most-named starter. Sell hand-dived and named-harbour scallops with the story attached.

    Impact: HighEffort: LowNext quarter
  2. 02

    Build a white fish range for mains

    Cod, hake and white fish mains rose from 2.3% to 5.6%: the clearest seafood riser. Portioned, with one sauce idea per cut.

    Impact: HighEffort: MediumNext two quarters
  3. 03

    Sell to chefs first

    The trade names seafood at 12.4% of everything it names, ahead of enthusiasts (10.9%). Sampling and kitchen visits beat consumer marketing.

    Impact: MediumEffort: LowOngoing
  4. 04

    Help one venue per district own a seafood dish

    Demand outside the top venue is thin and spread. Co-develop a signature with venues guests already name for one seafood dish.

    Impact: MediumEffort: MediumSix months
  5. 05

    Track the starter share quarterly

    Seafood starters are steady around a third. A fall below 30% would be the first sign of fatigue.

    Impact: LowEffort: LowQuarterly

06 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Scallop prices

Hand-dived scallop supply is weather and season dependent. A price spike would test how far the hero line can carry a menu price.

Prawns and langoustines

Prawns started high in the first quarter of fieldwork and have settled. A further fall would suggest fatigue with prawn starters.

White fish confirmation

Two more quarters at or above the recent share would turn the white fish move from clear to established.

Seafood outside Glasgow

The rest of Scotland indexes highly for scallops on few mentions. Targeted fieldwork on the coast would show whether that is real.

07 · Appendix

Method and sources

1,207dream starters
1,219dream mains
237seafood venue mentions
6quarters tracked

Starter and main shares use interviews with a codable answer for that course. Venue figures use mentions tied to a named venue. Quarters with fewer than 100 answers are shown faded.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.
