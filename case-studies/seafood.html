Scotland's seafood appetite

Written for a Scottish seafood supplier planning its foodservice range. The question: where does seafood sit on the dream menu, which dishes lead, what is rising and who owns it?

36.0% of dream starters are seafood
8.0% name scallops, the top starter
15.1% of dream mains are seafood
+3.3 pts white fish mains, a clear rise

The brief

A supplier of Scottish fish and shellfish wants to know which products to push to restaurants next year and where to find the venues that will take them. The report reads seafood across starters and mains, tracks it quarter by quarter, and names the venues guests associate with it.

What we found

Seafood owns the starter

434 of 1,207 dream starters (36.0%) are seafood, more than the next four groups combined. Scallops alone are 8.0%, the single most-named starter in the archive.

White fish is the riser

Cod, hake and white fish mains rose from 2.3% to 5.6% of dream mains, a clear move. Seafood mains overall rose from 12.1% to 16.0%, not yet a clear move but heading the same way.

The trade names it most

Seafood is 12.4% of everything hospitality professionals name, against 10.9% for food enthusiasts and 9.3% for content creators. Chefs and front of house are the strongest advocates.

A few venues carry it

Crabshakk Finnieston alone gathers 35 seafood mentions. Outside it, demand is spread across Leith, the West End and the coast, which leaves room for more venues to own a seafood dish.

Method and limits

Built from 1,238 Dream Menu interviews to 29 October 2026. Starter and main shares use the interviews with a codable answer for that course (1,207 starters, 1,219 mains). Venue figures use the mentions tied to a named venue.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, not a statistically representative survey. How Dream Menu works.
