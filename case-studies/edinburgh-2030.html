Edinburgh and Scotland to 2030

Edinburgh is the UK's first levy city and orders like a capital. A directional forecast for the capital and the rest of Scotland, from Dream Menu data and the latest market research.

Written for
A tourism body, investor or supplier with Scottish ambitions
The question

01 · Executive summary

The answer

In one sentence

Edinburgh is a stirred-drink, independent-led capital whose next five years will be shaped by the levy and by Leith, while the rest of Scotland's food story is coastal destinations; read this as direction, not a forecast, until the sample grows.

  • 346index

    A capital's appetite

  • 44%hidden gems

02 · The appetite

Edinburgh drinks like a capital

What the city orders more and less of than the rest of the archive.

Where Glasgow over-orders the margarita, Edinburgh over-orders the Negroni (index 346) and the martini. It also leans to sticky toffee pudding, white fish and steak. It is a stirred-drink, white-tablecloth, visitor-shaped appetite, and the next five years will be shaped by the one policy that is already live here and not yet in Glasgow: the visitor levy.

So what for a supplier

Two cities fifty minutes apart need two bar strategies. In Edinburgh, lead with vermouth, bitters, gin and the stirred classics; in Glasgow, with tequila, whisky and a short sour-led list.

03 · The districts

Leith is Edinburgh's Southside

Where guests name their hidden gems, and where the visitors are.

So what for investors and landlords

The Old Town has the most named venues (44) but the fewest mentions each (1.6): footfall without loyalty. Leith and Leith Walk are the reverse, and the levy's neighbourhood spending could speed them up. Edinburgh's Leith is what Glasgow's Southside is.

04 · The levy

The UK's first levy city is the test case

What is already live, what is coming and what the market is saying.

SignalWhat it means by 2030
Up to £50m a year from the levyThe UK's first city-wide visitor levy went live on 24 July 2026 (City of Edinburgh Council). By 2030 it will have funded around £200m of visitor-economy spending: events, cleaning, culture and housing.
About 60% of leasing take-up by local independentsFootfall up 2% and the highest hotel occupancy of Europe's major gateway cities (The Scotsman, August 2026). Independents are winning the space chains are leaving.
Ottolenghi is coming to the west endA London name choosing Edinburgh says the city is now on the national-brand map for food, not just for retail.
Chains leavingPizza Express closed two Leith sites; Las Iguanas left George Street. Casual-dining chains are the losers of the decade.

05 · The rest of Scotland

Outside the cities, the story is the coast

The towns guests name and the ingredient that could come next.

Away from the cities, guests name destinations rather than locals: seafood at Ullapool, Portree and Tarbert, hotel dining at Auchterarder and Crieff, and Aberdeen as the third city. Answers from the rest of Scotland over-index on prawns and langoustines and scallops. Scottish seaweed is being tipped as the next global food trend (The Scotsman), and kelp crofting is turning it into a coastal crop (SRUC kelpcrofting case study). If it happens, it will start on these coasts, on these menus.

So what for tourism bodies

The rest of Scotland's food story is a journey, not a high street. A seafood and destination-dining trail, funded as levies spread, is the obvious product.

06 · Outlook

Direction to 2030

Six themes, with direction and confidence.

ThemeDirection to 2030ConfidenceWhy
Stirred classics and aperitivoGrowingMediumEdinburgh over-indexes on the Negroni and martini; Whole Foods names "bitter done better" for 2027.
Independent restaurantsGrowingHighAbout 60% of take-up, as chains retreat from George Street and Leith.
Leith and Leith WalkGrowingMediumThe highest hidden-gem shares in the city and levy-funded culture in Leith.
Old Town visitor diningHoldingMediumLots of venues, few mentions each. The levy adds visitors, not loyalty.
Casual-dining chainsShrinkingHighClosures in Leith and George Street mirror the national figures.
Coastal and island seafood destinationsGrowingMediumAlready the most-named venues outside the cities; seaweed could add a second act.

Why "directional". Edinburgh supplies 382 answers to Glasgow's 1,293, and the rest of Scotland 142. That is enough to see the shape of the city's appetite, not enough to project it quarter by quarter. Edinburgh's growing share of venue mentions since late 2025 mostly reflects where Dream Menu interviews have reached, not a change in demand. A commission would add about 150 Edinburgh interviews to put the city on the same footing as Glasgow.

07 · Recommendations

What to do

Ranked by impact. The matrix shows where to start; the list says why.

  1. 01

    Run a separate Edinburgh bar programme

    Negroni index 346 and martini 280: vermouth, bitters and gin-led serves, not the Glasgow margarita list.

    Impact: HighEffort: LowNext quarter
  2. 02

    Back independents in Leith and Leith Walk

    The insider districts, with levy-funded culture arriving. Lease to food-led independents before rents follow.

    Impact: HighEffort: Medium12 to 24 months
  3. 03

    Use levy money for food beyond August

    Fund food events in the shoulder months so that the levy fills tables in March, not only during the festivals.

    Impact: MediumEffort: Medium2027

08 · Risks and signals

What would change our view

The signals to track over the next two quarters, and what each would mean for the recommendations.

Levy spending

If the first report shows levy money in events beyond August, raise the odds on Leith and the shoulder months.

Independent take-up

If the 60% share holds through 2027, the chain retreat is structural.

Negroni and martini share

A fall would suggest the stirred-drinks profile is a 2026 fashion rather than the city's character.

Other levies

Highland and other councils adopting levies would fund the coastal trail.

09 · Appendix

Method and sources

382Edinburgh answers
290Edinburgh venues named
180venues mapped to districts
142rest-of-Scotland answers

City index as in No. 12. Edinburgh districts use venues with a known address, assigned to the nearest of eight district centres within 1.3 km. Town mentions use venue locations from guest answers. This report is directional: no quarterly projections are made for Edinburgh because the quarterly samples are too small.

Dish categories were made more specific on 24 October 2026; each answer is coded to the dish itself with a parent group. A clear move means a two-proportion test between July to December 2025 and April 2026 onward came out at p < 0.05. Dream Menu is qualitative fieldwork with food-literate guests, chefs and hospitality staff, not a statistically representative survey of the population. How Dream Menu works.

Sources: City of Edinburgh Council, Edinburgh Magazine, August 2026, The Scotsman, August 2026, The Scotsman, SRUC kelpcrofting case study, Whole Foods Market, October 2026.
