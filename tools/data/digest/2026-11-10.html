Foodies, speaking for themselves

What 1,256 food-led guests are telling us, to 10 November 2026. Three findings and what to do about each.

Finding 1

Sides are where the menu is moving

18.3% of side answers now sit in a different food group than in late 2025. Sampling alone would move about 8.0%. Starters, mains and desserts are within chance.

What to do: Review the side list first. Change starters, mains and desserts for cost or quality, not to chase a trend.

Finding 2

13 dishes hold their share every quarter

Led by tiramisu, scallops and triple-cooked chips. Tiramisu alone takes 12.2% of dessert answers.

What to do: Protect these lines. Spend effort on how well they are made and priced, not on replacing them.

See the evidence

Finding 3

Cocktails lead; 7.9% choose alcohol-free

Cocktails take 41.1% of alcoholic drink answers. 7.9% answer the question with something alcohol-free. An early baseline from 151 answers since August 2026.

What to do: Give alcohol-free its own priced section, not a footnote, and lead the drinks list with house cocktails.

See the evidence

Outside check

Matcha: growth and backlash at once

The trend guests most want binned, named by 9.2% of all interviews. Tastewise, UK consumer and menu data (June 2026) reports the opposite: consumers choosing matcha lattes 33.5% more than a year earlier; matcha dishes on UK menus doubled.

What to do: Serve it well for the fans who want it, but don’t build a menu identity around it.
