Dream Menu: the cocktail’s grip is slipping, and two more findings

What 1,340 food-led guests are telling us, to 23 November 2026. Three findings and what to do about each.

1. The cocktail’s grip is slipping
Cocktails fell from 72.1% to 53.8% of drink picks since late 2025, a move far beyond chance. Whisky went from 3.1% to 9.6%, about three times as many picks. And 1 in 13 now answer “your dream alcoholic drink” with something alcohol-free.
What to do: Keep a tight house cocktail list, give a proper whisky and beer offer room, and price alcohol-free as a section, not a footnote.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis/change#drinks

2. Foodies’ favourite dessert is missing from 3 in 4 menus
Tiramisu is the dessert guests name most (12.1% of answers). We read 30 dessert menus from the venues guests rate highest: 76.7% leave it off.
What to do: If tiramisu is not on your list, trial it as a special this month.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis/demand#th-audit

3. The guides know the top 10. Foodies know the other 1,535
Foodies and critics agree on the very best: 9 of the 10 most-named venues hold a guide award or a place on a respected list. Below that, they part ways: 88.2% of the 1,740 places guests name are in no guide, award or list at all. That is where the next big names are.
What to do: Guides tell you who has arrived. Our venue map shows who guests rate before the guides catch up.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis/cities#critics

4. Chefs dream of seafood. Creators dream of chips
Seafood is 12.4% of what hospitality professionals choose, against 9.3% for food creators, who rate potatoes and fries (9.3%) as highly as seafood. The people who cook and the people who post want different plates.
What to do: Write the menu for your guests, and the specials board for the people who will photograph it.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis/demand

5. Irn Bru beats Coke. Pineapple on pizza is a dead heat
Asked to choose, 46.2% pick Irn Bru and 33.4% Coca-Cola. Pineapple on pizza splits the room: 49.6% yes, 50.4% no, from 1,254 answers.
What to do: Stock Irn Bru beside Coke. On pineapple, let the guest decide.
https://www.dream-menu.com/#debates

Outside check: The trend foodies most want gone is still growing
The trend guests most want binned, named by 9.2% of all interviews. Tastewise, UK consumer and menu data (June 2026) reports the opposite: consumers choosing matcha lattes 33.5% more than a year earlier; matcha dishes on UK menus doubled.
What to do: Serve it well for the fans who want it, but don’t build a menu identity around it.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis/trust
