Dream Menu: sides are where the menu is moving, and two more findings

What 1,266 food-led guests are telling us, to 15 November 2026. Three findings and what to do about each.

1. Sides are where the menu is moving
Sides are changing more than twice as fast as chance would explain: 17.9% of side answers have moved to a different food group since late 2025, against about 7.9% you would expect from the luck of who was asked. Starters, mains and desserts have moved no more than chance would explain.
What to do: Review the side list first. Change starters, mains and desserts for cost or quality, not to chase a trend.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis#changed

2. Tiramisu: 12.2% of guests, 23.3% of menus
We read the published menus of 45 of the venues guests name most. Tiramisu is the most-named dessert, yet it is on 23.3% of the 30 menus that print desserts. Other chocolate desserts are on 56.7%, for 2.7% of guests.
What to do: Check your dessert list against the 3 most-named (tiramisu, sticky toffee pudding, baked cheesecake) before adding anything new.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis#th-audit

3. Cocktails lead; 8.1% choose alcohol-free
Cocktails take 41.9% of alcoholic drink answers. 8.1% answer the question with something alcohol-free. An early baseline from 160 answers since August 2026.
What to do: Give alcohol-free its own priced section, not a footnote, and lead the drinks list with house cocktails.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis#drinks

Outside check: Matcha: growth and backlash at once
The trend guests most want binned, named by 9.2% of all interviews. Tastewise, UK consumer and menu data (June 2026) reports the opposite: consumers choosing matcha lattes 33.5% more than a year earlier; matcha dishes on UK menus doubled.
What to do: Serve it well for the fans who want it, but don’t build a menu identity around it.
https://www.dream-menu.com/analysis#compare
