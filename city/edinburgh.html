What Edinburgh names more than everywhere else

iHow to read this

Each figure is the gap, in percentage points, between a food’s share of Edinburgh’s venue mentions and its share everywhere else. Plus means Edinburgh names it more.

Shares keep a big city and a small one comparable. Gaps under a point or two are not worth acting on.
