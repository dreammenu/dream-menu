Where the evidence comes from

Guests have named venues in 130 cities and towns: 72 in the UK and 58 abroad. Glasgow and Edinburgh are each deep markets in their own right, so every finding can be read city by city. A city is deep once 30 or more guests have made 300 or more venue mentions there; below that it is an early signal, worth reading with care.
