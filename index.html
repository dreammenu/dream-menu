This quarter

What 1,340 foodies just told us

Six things the data says right now, from 1,340 interviews to 23 November 2026. Changes are tested against chance, not just counted; the grey card is an outside source, kept apart as a challenge to our own data. Each ends with what to do about it.

1
Drinks

The cocktail’s grip is slipping

Cocktails fell from 72.1% to 53.8% of drink picks since late 2025, a move far beyond chance. Whisky went from 3.1% to 9.6%, about three times as many picks. And 1 in 13 now answer “your dream alcoholic drink” with something alcohol-free.

What to doKeep a tight house cocktail list, give a proper whisky and beer offer room, and price alcohol-free as a section, not a footnote.

How drinks are moving →
2
Menus

Foodies’ favourite dessert is missing from 3 in 4 menus

Tiramisu is the dessert guests name most (12.1% of answers). We read 30 dessert menus from the venues guests rate highest: 76.7% leave it off.

What to doIf tiramisu is not on your list, trial it as a special this month.

What guests want against what menus list →
3
Venues

The guides know the top 10. Foodies know the other 1,535

Foodies and critics agree on the very best: 9 of the 10 most-named venues hold a guide award or a place on a respected list. Below that, they part ways: 88.2% of the 1,740 places guests name are in no guide, award or list at all. That is where the next big names are.

What to doGuides tell you who has arrived. Our venue map shows who guests rate before the guides catch up.

Guests and the guides →
4
Who orders what

Chefs dream of seafood. Creators dream of chips

Seafood is 12.4% of what hospitality professionals choose, against 9.3% for food creators, who rate potatoes and fries (9.3%) as highly as seafood. The people who cook and the people who post want different plates.

What to doWrite the menu for your guests, and the specials board for the people who will photograph it.

What guests want, by who they are →
5
The debates

Irn Bru beats Coke. Pineapple on pizza is a dead heat

Asked to choose, 46.2% pick Irn Bru and 33.4% Coca-Cola. Pineapple on pizza splits the room: 49.6% yes, 50.4% no, from 1,254 answers.

What to doStock Irn Bru beside Coke. On pineapple, let the guest decide.

Outside check

The trend foodies most want gone is still growing

The trend guests most want binned, named by 9.2% of all interviews. Tastewise, UK consumer and menu data (June 2026) reports the opposite: consumers choosing matcha lattes 33.5% more than a year earlier; matcha dishes on UK menus doubled.

What to doServe it well for the fans who want it, but don’t build a menu identity around it.

The dishes people choose

For each course, the share of guests whose dream dish is that specific dish. Scallops lead the starters, tiramisu and sticky toffee pudding the desserts, and skinny fries only just beat triple-cooked chips. Named this precisely, no single dish takes more than one answer in eight.

iHow to read this

Each card is one course. The percentage is the share of that course’s answers naming the dish; every bar on every card uses the same scale, so a longer bar is a bigger share whichever course it is in.

“Steak” counts every steak once, whatever the cut. Answers that fit no dish are left out, so shares are of answers we could code.
