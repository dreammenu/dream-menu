Advice for anyone who cooks

Take pride in the ingredients you use and find wee rituals that make cooking enjoyable, so that spending time creating something delicious and nutritious doesn't feel…
Don’t be afraid to take risks with your ingredients!
Go with taste and less with measurements. Taste as you go and if it works great! Our industry heavily relies on 100g of this and 50g of that. We try to ignore as much of…
Taste everything, trial & error and just have fun!
Don’t overcomplicate it. Good ingredients, treated simply, win every time. And never skimp on the crumble topping!
Invest in a good temperature probe (I like the thermopen.)
Caramelise your sofrito
Don’t forget salt
Experiment, try new things, don't be shy, just try.
Butter, loads of butter, dont be scared of butter.
Always try to use the best ingridients for your dish.. doesn't matter if is italian,french or any other cuisine.. the quality of the products makes the difference at the…
Take your time focus on one thing you like, less is definitely more. Depth over bulk!
