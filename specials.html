Only at the Fringe

I witnessed Karen Gillan birth Elf Lyons on stage, before they delivered a hack 10 minute club set at Stamptown. For sure not something you see every day.
My very first Fringe gig, back in 2022, was in the basement of a Mexican takeaway. Halfway through my set, a chef walked in front of me carrying a huge donner meat skewer.
Having two people at my show and getting 50 pounds at the end of doing a whole hour and the opposite of having a full house a few nights earlier and not even getting 15 quid at the end
