What is on your dream menu?

Chefs, owners, front of house, suppliers, writers and people who simply love eating out: everyone gets the same question, and every answer goes on the map.

@dream.menu

How it works

  1. Pick a starter, main, side, dessert and drinks
  2. Tell us where each one is best, anywhere in the world
  3. Add a hidden gem or the next place you want to try
  4. We publish your menu and add your venues to the map

Get involved

Interviews run through Instagram. Send a message to @dream.menu and we will send you the questions. It takes about ten minutes.

We also run themed specials, such as Dream Fringe during the Edinburgh Festival, Dream Cocktail and Dream Sandwich. Follow the account to hear when the next one opens.
