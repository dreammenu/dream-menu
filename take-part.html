What is on your dream menu?

Chefs, owners, front of house, suppliers, writers and people who simply love eating out, wherever you are: everyone gets the same question, and every answer goes on the map.

Start the form @dream.menu

How it works

  1. Pick a starter, main, side, dessert and drinks
  2. Tell us where each one is best, anywhere in the world
  3. Add a hidden gem or the next place you want to try
  4. We publish your menu and add your venues to the map

Prefer Instagram?

Get your menu featured

Tick the box on the form to have your menu published with your name, and it can appear on a guest menus page here and on our Instagram. Leave it unticked and your answers are counted anonymously in the trends.

We also run themed specials, such as Dream Fringe during the Edinburgh Festival, Dream Cocktail and Dream Sandwich. Follow the account to hear when the next one opens.
