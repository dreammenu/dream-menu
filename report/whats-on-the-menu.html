What's on the dream menu

People asked for an ideal rather than a recent meal, so the answers show what they really want. The patterns across 1,221 interviews are consistent enough to read as direction, not just opinion.

What the tracker says

The classics are holding. Seafood starters and steak both rose through 2026, though not by enough to call a clear move, and neither is losing ground. The clear losses are in fries (skinny fries and loaded fries most of all), fried and burger-style sides, chicken starters and baked cheesecake. Sides are moving toward greens and vegetables. Among people naming an alcoholic drink, cocktails have lost a lot of ground to whisky, lager and beer.

For suppliers and operators: a staple losing share in what people say they want is worth watching before it shows in reorder data.

Starters

Seafood leads with 430 of 1,191 starter answers (36%). Scallops are the single most named starter (96); 28 named Crabshakk and scallops together, the most-cited venue and dish pairing in the dataset. Haggis bon bons (32), steak tartare (35) and bread, focaccia or sourdough (43) follow behind the seafood.

FormatWhat to do
ScallopsCrabshakk owns this. Challenge with a distinct identity, not the same format.
Focaccia / sourdoughNamed grain, house fermentation, house butter: the difference between a commodity and a signature.
Haggis bon bons32 mentions, no definitive venue. Name the black pudding supplier; make your own chutney.
BurrataStill wanted but needs a reason to exist. "Burrata alone is 2019."

Mains

Steak and beef is the defining main: 309 answers (26%), rising through 2026. People ask for a steak with a story: the cut, the farm, the ageing. Pasta and Italian (103) comes with high literacy; diners know if it is made in-house.

FormatWhat to do
SteakExplain the animal, the farm and the ageing. Provenance sells premium cuts.
Fresh pasta"House pasta" says more than "linguine". The process is part of the value.
Whole fishThe format gap: few Glasgow venues serve a whole turbot or sea bass.
Sunday roast42 dream mains, ritual language, no city-centre destination. Price it at £30+.
Chicken67 mentions when cooked with intent: spatchcock, confit, caramelised, Kyiv.
CurryRegional specificity is the differentiator. "Chettinad lamb", not "curry".

Sides

Fries lead (260) but are losing share fast: potatoes and fries as a group is down 12.7 points, the largest clear move in any food course. The versions that earn advocacy are distinctive: spiced, hand-cut in beef dripping. Potato done with care (136) holds up, and greens are rising. Mac and cheese (56) is the most underserved side in quality dining.

FormatWhat to do
FriesBuild a fries programme that justifies the order.
Truffle friesWanted by 48, tired of by 16. A real truffle product, named, is still interesting; flavouring spray is not.
Mac and cheeseNamed cheese, house béchamel, a crisp top. Easy to add, earns outsized comment.

Desserts

Tiramisu (145) and sticky toffee pudding (130) are the two most named desserts by some distance. Tiramisu is won by kitchens with a family recipe; sticky toffee pudding is the emotional dessert. Ice cream and gelato (140) is spread across many operators with no single destination.

FormatWhat to do
Dessert as revenueWhen guests leave for dessert elsewhere, another operator gets the final spend and the final memory.
Sticky toffee puddingThe dessert that makes people come back.
TiramisuNot a menu filler. The ones people remember have a cultural relationship with the dish.

Drinks

Margaritas (104) and martinis (63) lead the cocktails, but cocktails are losing share to whisky, lager and beer. Guinness (40) is named more often than Aperol Spritz (24) or espresso martini (20).

FormatWhat to do
MargaritaThree variants: classic, spicy, seasonal. Name the tequila.
MartiniOwn one variation with a story rather than seven generic ones.
SpritzLead with Hugo or a house spritz; Aperol as an option, not the lead.
GuinnessPour it properly, at temperature. People notice a bad pour.
No / lowAt least four designed non-alcoholic drinks, named with the same care as cocktails.
WineEdging up. A clearly labelled natural section with three or four by the glass.
The signature drink as a commercial asset. Sebb's is named somewhere by 51 respondents, more than any other venue, and 21 of them mention its margaritas. A drink built with real intention (a named spirit, a specific flavour story) becomes a reason to visit. Meanwhile the share of 18 to 35 year olds who drink falls every year: operators building serious no/low lists now will have a head start that is hard to copy.

Trend fatigue: what to stop doing

Unprompted answers to "Do you have a food trend you wish would quietly disappear?" (1,164 answering). AI-generated food imagery is also called out unprompted: these are the people who make food content, and they can spot it.
